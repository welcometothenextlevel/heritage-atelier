The Pappou & Yiayia Collection

For the one who says he needs nothing

Greek heritage kitchen and family gifts, personalised in the language the family actually uses.

Why this collection exists

He needs nothing. He means everything.

Ask a Greek grandfather what he wants for his name day and he will tell you to save your money. Then he will spend the afternoon making enough food for fourteen people who said they were not hungry.

This collection is for that. Kitchen things, family things, with his name on them — Pappou George, Chef of Everything — because the kitchen is where he says the things he does not say anywhere else.

Language

Greek script or English, your call

Every piece in this collection can be set in Greek or English. We will typeset Παππού and Γιαγιά properly, with the accents where they belong, and send a proof before anything is cut or engraved.

If you are unsure of the spelling your family uses, send us a photo of how it is written on a card. We have read worse handwriting.
