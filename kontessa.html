The Soft Romance Collection

A bag that takes eleven days

Kontessa began with one request: a bag that looked like it had been inherited rather than bought. The Soft Romance Collection is the answer — blush, cream and ivory cotton, worked stitch by stitch, finished with solid antique gold hardware and a lining cut from silk twill.

Nothing is dyed to trend. The palette is the one that suits a wardrobe already in the cupboard, and it does not change from season to season.

The process

Crocheted, blocked, lined, closed

  • YarnLong staple Egyptian cotton and a soft merino bouclé, both chosen for how they hold a shape after a full day out.
  • HardwareSolid brass clasps and rings in an antique gold finish. No plating that lifts at the edges.
  • LiningSilk twill, cut and hand stitched in, with one interior pocket sized for a phone.
  • TimingNine to fourteen days from the day the colourway is confirmed. Longer in November.
  • PackingA black ribbon gift box, a dust bag, and a card written by hand if you ask for one.
Personalisation

Her initials, inside the lining

Two or three letters, hand embroidered into the silk where only she will see them. Added at no cost, on any Kontessa piece, if you send them through with the order.
