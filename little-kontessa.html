Little Kontessa

The gift that outlives the size

Babies grow out of everything in eleven weeks. So Little Kontessa is built around the two or three pieces that get kept instead: the blanket from the hospital bed, the bodysuit with Nouna's name on it, the rattle that ends up on a shelf in a grown up's house.

Soft cottons, muted colourways, and wording in Greek or English — whichever the family actually says out loud.

How personalisation works

Three decisions, then we begin

  • One — choose the nameThe baby's name, or the giver's title: Nouna, Yiayia, Theia, Pappou. In Greek script or English, your choice.
  • Two — choose the colourwayCream, ivory, blush, oat or sage thread on white or oat cotton. We send a photo of the thread against the fabric before stitching.
  • Three — we make it to orderNothing is pre printed. The piece is cut, embroidered and pressed after you approve the proof.

Allow seven to ten days, plus post. Christening deadlines are worth telling us about early.

Sizing

Newborn through to 18–24 months

Bodysuits and tees run newborn, 0–3, 3–6, 6–12, 12–18 and 18–24 months. Blankets are a single pram size, roughly 75 by 100 centimetres. If you are buying ahead for a christening, order one size up — almost everyone wishes they had.
