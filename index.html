Nothing here exists until somebody asks for it

We do not hold stock. Each bag is crocheted, each bodysuit embroidered and each board engraved after the order is placed, with the name, the language and the colourway chosen by the person giving it.

That is slower. It is also the only way a gift ends up in a drawer thirty years later, still meaning something.

Every piece is made here, by hand, in a home studio in Sydney. There is no factory and no wholesale line. When you send a name through, it is written on a card and worked into the piece by the same person who answers the email.
